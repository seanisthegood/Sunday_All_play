Sources & calculation rules

Scores: every_team_weekly_scores.csv, referenced by fantasy_2026.ipynb. Actual records and points against: owner-supplied standings, weeks 1–7. This is a fixed snapshot, not a live feed.

Without byes includes only rows marked Played. Bye teams are excluded as both row team and opponent. With byes includes every recorded score. Points for and weekly averages use the selected pool; actual records and points against remain unchanged. Bye scores are still shown in weekly row labels for reference.

All-play compares each eligible score with every other eligible score in the same week, excluding self. Equal scores count as ties. Season cells sum those weekly W–L–T results, rather than comparing total season points. Click any standings heading to sort; click again to reverse. Actual and all-play records sort by win rate (ties count as half a win). Equal values use points for, then team name. This is an analytical ranking, not an official league tiebreaker.

The notebook creates all_play_df_record_bye but later displays all_play_df. The app recalculates from source scores and validates against saved notebook records and the detailed matchup CSV.
